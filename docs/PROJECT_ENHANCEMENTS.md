## 💡 Suggested Enhancements

### 1. **Budgeting & Goals** 🎯
**What:** Set monthly budgets for each expense category and track spending
- Set budget limits (e.g., Food: ₹10,000/month)
- Visual progress bars showing spent vs budget
- Alerts when approaching or exceeding budget
- Monthly budget vs actual reports

**Why:** Helps control spending and achieve financial goals

**Complexity:** Medium
**Impact:** High

---

### 2. **Recurring Transactions** 🔄
**What:** Automate regular income/expenses
- Mark transactions as recurring (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Auto-create transactions on schedule
- Edit/delete recurring series
- Examples: Salary, rent, subscriptions, EMIs

**Why:** Saves time and ensures you don't forget regular payments

**Complexity:** Medium
**Impact:** High

---

### 3. **Bill Reminders & Alerts** 🔔
**What:** Never miss a payment deadline
- Add bills with due dates
- Browser notifications before due date
- Mark bills as paid
- Track payment history

**Why:** Avoid late fees and maintain good credit

**Complexity:** Low-Medium
**Impact:** High

---

### 4. **Multi-Currency Support** 💱
**What:** Track accounts in different currencies
- Add accounts in USD, EUR, etc.
- Auto-convert to base currency (INR)
- Exchange rate tracking
- Multi-currency reports

**Why:** Useful for foreign investments, travel, or international income

**Complexity:** Medium
**Impact:** Medium (depends on your needs)

**Impact:** High
